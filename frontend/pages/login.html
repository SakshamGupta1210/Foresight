About

Problem Statement

QR codes make it easy to access websites and services, but users cannot easily tell where a QR code will take them before scanning it. This creates a risk of landing on fake websites, phishing pages, or fraudulent links.

Proposed Solution

FORESIGHT is a web-based security system that analyzes QR code destinations, traces redirects, and identifies potential security risks. It generates a risk score and provides clear explanations of detected threats, helping users make informed decisions before accessing a link.

Objectives

  1. To develop a web-based system that scans QR codes and extracts their destination URLs for security analysis.
  2. To assess QR code destinations by analyzing URL patterns, redirects, and suspicious indicators, and generate an understandable risk score with supporting reasons.
  3. To provide users with clear security warnings and scan history, helping them make informed decisions before accessing potentially unsafe links.
